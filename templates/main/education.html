Cybersecurity Education Hub

Five core units aligned with the Indian cybersecurity syllabus. Stay informed, stay protected.

Unit 1 – Cybersecurity Fundamentals Foundation
🔐

CIA Triad

The three pillars of information security: Confidentiality (data accessible only to authorised users), Integrity (data is accurate and unaltered), and Availability (systems are accessible when needed).

ConfidentialityIntegrityAvailability
🦠

Types of Cyber Threats

Major threat categories include Malware (viruses, ransomware, spyware), Phishing (deceptive communications), Social Engineering (psychological manipulation), and MITM attacks (intercepting communications).

MalwarePhishingRansomware
🛡️

Defence Mechanisms

Key controls: Firewalls, Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Encryption (AES-256, TLS), and Security patching. Defence-in-depth uses multiple layered controls.

FirewallMFAEncryption
🔑

Password & Credential Security

Use strong, unique passwords (≥12 chars, mixed case, numbers, symbols). Enable MFA everywhere. Use a password manager. Never reuse passwords across services. Hashing algorithms like bcrypt/PBKDF2 protect stored passwords.

PasswordsMFAPBKDF2
Unit 2 – Cybercrime & Indian Cyber Law Legal
⚖️

Information Technology Act, 2000

India's primary cyber law. Key sections: Section 43 – Unauthorised computer access; Section 66 – Computer-related offences; Section 66C – Identity theft; Section 66D – Cheating by impersonation; Section 67 – Obscene content online.

IT Act 2000S.66CS.66D
🚔

Types of Cybercrime

Financial fraud (UPI/OTP/banking scams), Identity theft, Cyber bullying, Ransomware, Sextortion, Online job scams, and Investment fraud. All are punishable under the IT Act and IPC.

IT ActIPCFraud
📞

How to Report Cybercrime

1. Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately.
2. File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
3. Visit local police station for FIR under IT Act / IPC.
4. Preserve all screenshots, call records, and bank statements as evidence.

1930 Helplinecybercrime.gov.in
🏛️

CERT-In & DPDP Act

CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team) handles national cybersecurity incidents. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP) governs how organisations process personal data — consent-based, purpose-limited, with data principal rights.

CERT-InDPDP 2023Data Rights
Unit 3 – Social Media Security Digital Safety
📱

Social Engineering via Social Media

Attackers use fake profiles, romance scams, impersonation, and urgent messages to extract money or credentials. Red flags: Requests for money, unsolicited links, too-good-to-be-true offers, pressure tactics.

Fake ProfilesRomance ScamImpersonation
🔒

Account Security Best Practices

Enable 2FA/MFA on all social accounts. Review app permissions regularly. Use strong unique passwords. Check login activity. Never click unverified links in DMs. Verify unusual requests from "friends" through another channel.

2FAApp PermissionsLogin Alerts
🔍

Privacy Settings & Data Minimisation

Limit profile visibility to friends only. Avoid sharing phone numbers, home addresses, or travel plans publicly. Understand each platform's data collection policy. Use Google's Data Export and Facebook Privacy Check-up tools.

Privacy SettingsData Minimisation
🤖

AI-Generated Content & Deepfakes

AI can generate convincing fake videos, voice clones, and images. Signs of deepfakes: unnatural eye movement, mismatched audio sync, blurry edges. Use reverse image search, verify via official channels, and use tools like InVID.

DeepfakesVoice CloningVerification
Unit 4 – Digital Payment Fraud Prevention High Priority
💳

UPI & Mobile Banking Fraud

Common scams: OTP sharing, fake QR codes, UPI payment requests disguised as money receipts, SIM swap fraud, fake bank customer service calls. Remember: receiving money requires NO PIN entry.

UPIOTP FraudSIM Swap
🏦

Safe Banking Practices

Always verify IFSC/account details before transferring. Use RBI-regulated payment apps only. Enable transaction alerts via SMS and email. Set daily transfer limits. Immediately block card/account if compromised via bank's official number.

RBI GuidelinesTransfer LimitsAlerts
🛒

E-Commerce & Online Shopping Fraud

Verify seller ratings, use trusted platforms. Avoid advance payment for unknown sellers. Check HTTPS and authentic domain names. Use credit cards (chargeback protection) over debit cards. Report fraud to the platform and local consumer court.

E-CommerceHTTPSChargeback
📲

Vishing & Smishing Attacks

Vishing (voice phishing) – callers impersonate bank/IT/police officials. Smishing (SMS phishing) – fraudulent SMS with malicious links. Never act on urgency from unknown callers. Verify by calling the official number independently.

VishingSmishingVerification
Unit 5 – Device & Endpoint Security Technical
📡

Secure Wi-Fi & Network Usage

Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking. Use WPA3 encryption at home. Change default router credentials. Use a VPN on untrusted networks. Disable Wi-Fi auto-connect. Enable firewall on all devices. Segment IoT devices on separate network.

WPA3VPNFirewall
📱

Mobile Device Security

Enable full-disk encryption. Use biometrics + PIN. Install apps from official stores only. Review permissions before installing. Keep OS and apps updated. Enable remote wipe (Find My Device / Google Find My). Avoid rooting/jailbreaking.

EncryptionApp StoresRemote Wipe
💻

PC & Laptop Hardening

Keep Windows/macOS updated. Use licensed antivirus. Enable Windows Defender / Gatekeeper. Encrypt drives with BitLocker/FileVault. Use standard user account for daily tasks. Disable unnecessary services and remote desktop when not in use.

AntivirusBitLockerPatching
💾

Data Backup & Recovery

Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies of data, on 2 different media types, with 1 offsite/cloud backup. Test restores regularly. Encrypt backups. Protects against ransomware, hardware failure, and accidental deletion.

3-2-1 BackupRansomware DefenceCloud Backup
