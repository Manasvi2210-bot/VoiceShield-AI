🎣 Module 1: Phishing & Social Engineering Human Factor Defense

Core Concept: Phishing exploits human psychological biases (authority, scarcity, curiosity, and fear) rather than technical software bugs. Attackers impersonate trusted brands, colleagues, or executives to steal credentials or induce unauthorized payments.

Key Takeaway & Rule of Thumb:
Never approve wire transfers, share one-time passcodes (OTPs), or click links in unsolicited urgent emails. Always verify the communication through a trusted secondary channel (out-of-band phone call or internal chat).
🦠 Module 2: Ransomware & Malware Prevention Endpoint Protection

Core Concept: Ransomware encrypts user files and databases using asymmetric cryptography, demanding cryptocurrency payments for decryption. Modern variants utilize double extortion—threatening public disclosure of exfiltrated data if the ransom is not paid.

The 3-2-1 Backup Defense:
Maintain 3 copies of critical data across 2 different media types, with at least 1 copy stored off-site, offline, or in an immutable cloud bucket that cannot be reached from the local network.
🔑 Module 3: Multi-Factor Authentication & Password Hygiene Identity Security

Core Concept: Passwords alone are vulnerable to credential stuffing, dictionary guessing, and database breaches. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) requires two or more independent factors: Something you know (password), Something you have (authenticator app / FIDO2 key), or Something you are (biometrics).

Phishing-Resistant MFA:
Hardware security keys (FIDO2 / WebAuthn) bind the cryptographic challenge to the legitimate domain origin, completely neutralizing reverse-proxy phishing attacks that bypass SMS codes.
🌐 Module 4: Safe Browsing & Suspicious Link Analysis Web Defense

Core Concept: An HTTPS padlock only indicates that the connection between your browser and the remote server is encrypted—it does not prove the website is honest or safe. Phishing sites frequently register free TLS certificates.

Domain Inspection Rule:
Read the fully qualified domain name backwards from the first single forward slash. For example, in https://paypal.com.verify-security.xyz/login, the real destination domain is verify-security.xyz, not PayPal!
🔒 Module 5: Data Privacy & Safe Information Sharing Regulatory Compliance

Core Concept: Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN, date of birth, and home addresses are weaponized by identity thieves. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, organizations are legally mandated to implement technical and organizational security safeguards.

Principle of Data Minimization:
Only collect and disclose the exact data points strictly necessary for the specified operational purpose. Mask identity identifiers wherever full disclosure is not required by law.
📶 Module 6: Public Wi-Fi & Mobile Device Security Network Protection

Core Concept: Open public Wi-Fi networks (cafes, airports, transit) lack packet isolation, allowing adversaries to launch rogue "Evil Twin" hotspots or perform ARP spoofing to inspect unencrypted traffic and redirect DNS requests.

Defensive Best Practice:
Use corporate or trusted Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) when connecting through public networks, disable automatic Wi-Fi reconnect, and enforce Full Disk Encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) on all portable laptops.
🚨 Module 7: Cyber Incident Reporting & Rapid Response Regulatory & Incident Triage

Core Concept: Rapid containment determines whether an incident remains an isolated compromise or escalates into catastrophic data exfiltration. Prompt reporting enables financial freeze windows and statutory compliance.

Crucial Contact Channels:
• Citizen Financial Fraud Helpline 1930: Immediate call to freeze fraudulent UPI/banking disbursements.
• National Cyber Crime Portal: File formal police complaints at cybercrime.gov.in.
• CERT-In Directions: Organizations must report qualifying incidents within 6 hours to incident@cert-in.org.in.
🎙️ Module 8: Voice-Cloning Impersonation Awareness Emerging Generative Threats

Core Concept: Neural speech synthesis models can clone a speaker's vocal timbre with short audio samples harvested from speeches or social media. Attackers combine synthetic voices with caller ID spoofing to execute high-impact vishing attacks against finance teams.

Zero-Trust Vocal Verification:
Never rely on voice familiarity alone to authorize money transfers or reset root credentials. Establish a mandatory verbal challenge passphrase or hang up and call back over verified internal extensions.
