Privacy Notice

How VoiceShield AI handles your data. Last updated: October 2026.

✅ Core Privacy Commitment

Audio files you upload are permanently deleted from the server immediately after analysis — whether the analysis succeeds or fails. We never share your data with third parties.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: username, email address, password hash (PBKDF2-SHA256).
  • Incident data: titles, categories, transcripts, risk scores, acoustic measurements — all linked to your account.
  • Audio files: uploaded temporarily for transcription and acoustic analysis, then immediately deleted.
  • We do NOT collect: IP addresses for logging, browser fingerprints, or marketing data.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To transcribe audio locally using the Whisper AI model (offline, not sent to any external API).
  • To analyse transcript text for known scam phrases and compute a risk score.
  • To compute acoustic signal measurements (clipping, spectral flatness, SNR).
  • To display your personal incident history — visible only to you.

3. Audio File Deletion

Audio files are saved temporarily to the uploads/ folder only during the analysis pipeline. They are deleted using os.remove() as the final step of every analysis — even if transcription or measurement fails. No audio data is retained in the database; only the transcript text (if successful) is stored.

4. Transcription – Local Processing

Speech-to-text is performed by OpenAI Whisper running locally on this server. Your audio is never sent to OpenAI servers or any external API. If Whisper is unavailable, you will see an "unavailable" status — we will never fabricate a transcript.

5. Acoustic Measurements – Disclaimer

Acoustic figures (clipping ratio, spectral flatness, SNR) are signal quality measurements only. They do not determine whether audio is AI-generated or authentic. Do not use these measurements as legal evidence of voice deepfakes.

6. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed with Werkzeug's PBKDF2-SHA256 — never stored in plaintext.
  • Secret keys are loaded from environment variables, not hardcoded.
  • All routes that access incident data verify ownership against the logged-in user (server-side authorisation).
  • File uploads are validated for extension and size (max 50 MB).
  • Filenames are sanitised with werkzeug.utils.secure_filename and prefixed with a UUID.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: View all your incidents at any time in the History page.
  • Delete: Delete individual incidents from the History or Detail pages.
  • Export: Download all your data as CSV or PDF.
  • Account deletion: Contact the administrator to fully delete your account and all associated data.

8. Indian Law Compliance

This application is designed with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) in mind, including purpose limitation, data minimisation, and data subject rights. Cybercrime incidents you record may be shared with law enforcement — please do so at cybercrime.gov.in.

9. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact the system administrator or visit cybercrime.gov.in. Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930.
