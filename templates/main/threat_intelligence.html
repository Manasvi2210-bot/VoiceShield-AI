CRITICAL MITRE ATT&CK T1566.001
Reviewed: Oct 2026 | Source: CISA / CERT-In

Spear Phishing & Business Email Compromise (BEC)

Targeted social-engineering emails crafted with sender-spoofing or lookalike typosquat domains to deceive finance personnel into redirecting vendor payments or surrendering corporate SSO credentials.

TYPICAL INDICATORS (IoCs)

  • Display-Name Spoofing: Executive name with external domain (ceo-desk@secure-mail-update.net)
  • Urgent Payment Mod: Immediate invoice rerouting requests bypassing dual-signoff protocols
  • Lookalike URL: Punycode or character substitution (e.g. micros0ft-login.com)

POTENTIAL IMPACT

Unauthorized fund transfers, account takeover, exfiltration of corporate communications, regulatory disclosure liabilities under IT Act Sec 43A / DPDP Act 2023.

DEFENSIVE MITIGATIONS

  • Enforce SPF, DKIM, and DMARC with strict p=reject enforcement policies.
  • Mandate out-of-band secondary channel confirmation for any bank account number change.
  • Deploy FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware MFA resistant to adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) reverse proxies.
CRITICAL MITRE ATT&CK T1486
Reviewed: Oct 2026 | Source: CISA Alert AA23-325A

Double-Extortion Ransomware Campaigns

Adversaries breach perimeter network via exposed RDP/VPN or phishing, exfiltrate confidential gigabytes to cloud storage for extortion leverage, and deploy AES-256/ChaCha20 encryption across all domain shares.

TYPICAL INDICATORS (IoCs)

  • VSS Tampering: Execution of vssadmin.exe delete shadows /all /quiet
  • Data Stage Tooling: Unapproved execution of Rclone, MegaSync, or PsExec on servers
  • Mass File Rename: Rapid creation of appended extensions (e.g. .locked) and ransom notes

POTENTIAL IMPACT

Total operational paralysis, irreversible loss of proprietary customer data, mandatory CERT-In 6-hour cybersecurity incident reporting compliance.

DEFENSIVE MITIGATIONS

  • Maintain immutable, offline air-gapped backups tested via quarterly bare-metal restores.
  • Implement zero-trust network segmentation to prevent lateral movement across server VLANs.
  • Disable public internet-facing RDP (TCP 3389) and require MFA-protected VPN tunnels.
HIGH MITRE ATT&CK T1598.003
Reviewed: Oct 2026 | Source: FBI IC3 Alert / VoiceShield Lab

Voice-Cloning & AI-Assisted Vishing Schemes

Threat actors harvest 10-30 seconds of high-fidelity executive speech from public podcasts, webinars, or media interviews to synthesize urgent phone requests instructing junior staff to authorize emergency wire transfers.

TYPICAL INDICATORS (IoCs)

  • Extreme Emotional Urgency: Claims of critical acquisition deadline, hostage situation, or severe legal penalty
  • Audio Artifacts: Monotone prosody, unnatural breath timing, and abrupt spectral cutoff past 4 kHz
  • Unconventional Routing: Caller insists on using non-corporate WhatsApp, Telegram, or burner mobile numbers

POTENTIAL IMPACT

High-value fraudulent disbursements, bypass of voice biometric authentication in banking call centers, severe corporate reputational damage.

DEFENSIVE MITIGATIONS

  • Establish agreed-upon verbal verification code words or challenge-response questions for high-value requests.
  • Strict callback policy: hang up and dial back the executive using the official corporate directory number.
  • Never permit single-person authorization for wire transfers regardless of the caller's stated seniority.
HIGH MITRE ATT&CK T1110.003
Reviewed: Oct 2026 | Source: OWASP Top 10 A07 / NIST 800-63B

Automated Credential Stuffing & Password Spraying

Adversaries leverage leaked breach databases (billions of username:password pairs) through automated botnets to systematically test against customer login portals, capitalizing on widespread cross-site password reuse.

TYPICAL INDICATORS (IoCs)

  • Distributed Traffic: Thousands of login attempts per hour distributed across residential proxy IP pools
  • Single Password Spray: Testing one common password (e.g. Winter2026!) across all known employee accounts to evade lockout
  • High Failure Ratio: Sudden spike in HTTP 401/403 responses on authentication endpoints

POTENTIAL IMPACT

Mass account takeover, unauthorized customer reward draining, compromised cloud portal management consoles, severe privacy audit penalties.

DEFENSIVE MITIGATIONS

  • Implement progressive rate limiting, IP reputation scoring, and CAPTCHA challenges on authentication routes.
  • Mandate phishing-resistant Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all administrative and user portals.
  • Deploy password compromise screening (checking new passwords against known compromised password corpora).
HIGH MITRE ATT&CK T1530
Reviewed: Oct 2026 | Source: Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) / CERT-In

Unauthenticated Cloud Storage & API Data Exposure

Improper access control lists (ACLs) or unauthenticated REST APIs allow internet scanners and threat actors to download database backups, customer PII, or internal documents without requiring authentication.

TYPICAL INDICATORS (IoCs)

  • Public Bucket ACL: S3 buckets or Azure blobs configured with AllUsers:READ permissions
  • Hardcoded Secrets: API tokens, AWS secret keys, or database connection strings committed to public GitHub repositories
  • Open Elasticsearch/MongoDB: Unauthenticated database ports (9200, 27017) exposed directly to WAN

POTENTIAL IMPACT

Massive statutory penalties under DPDP Act 2023 (up to ₹250 crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards), catastrophic customer trust erosion.

DEFENSIVE MITIGATIONS

  • Enforce Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM) with automated detection of public storage buckets.
  • Use automated pre-commit secret scanners (e.g. git-secrets, Trufflehog) in CI/CD pipelines.
  • Mandate encryption-at-rest with customer-managed keys (CMK) across all database and storage volumes.
MEDIUM MITRE ATT&CK T1598.002
Reviewed: Oct 2026 | Source: Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)

Messaging App Pretexting & Utility Disconnection Scams

Scammers send messages via WhatsApp or SMS claiming imminent electricity disconnection, unpaid traffic challans, or courier delivery address updates, directing victims to install malicious APK files or remote-desktop utilities (AnyDesk, TeamViewer).

TYPICAL INDICATORS (IoCs)

  • Threat of Disconnection: Claims of immediate service cutoff at 9:30 PM tonight unless an app is installed
  • Sideloaded APK Link: URL ending in .apk hosted on free hosting services (Firebase, MediaFire)
  • Screen Share Demand: Instructions to share a 9-digit remote control code to “assist with bill verification”

POTENTIAL IMPACT

Total remote takeover of victim's smartphone, interception of SMS OTPs, unauthorized UPI transactions, and contact list extortion.

DEFENSIVE MITIGATIONS

  • Educate personnel never to sideload APKs or grant Accessibility permissions to unverified applications.
  • Never download remote desktop software at the instruction of an unknown caller or messaging contact.
  • Verify utility payment status directly through the provider's official portal or mobile app.
